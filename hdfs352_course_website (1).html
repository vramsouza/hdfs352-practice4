Policies

Academic Honesty and Plagiarism

Work must be your own (or your team's). Always acknowledge when you build on someone else’s ideas, including classmates, the professor, or authors. Plagiarism—passing off others' ideas or writings as your own—is not tolerated.

Disability Accommodations

If you require accommodations, consult with the instructor during the first week of class and contact the KOKUA Program at (808) 956-7511 or (808) 956-7612.

Attendance

Attendance is graded (16 points total). A zero is given for missed classes regardless of the reason. Repeated tardiness or partial attendance will result in a loss of points. Up to 3 absences can be made up via an essay assignment upon email request.

Grievance Policy

Students must meet with the instructor first to resolve disputes. If equitable treatment is not received, contact HDFS program coordinator Prof. Rheta Kuwahara for mediation.

Student Conduct

Students are expected to be responsible in relationships with others and to respect the interests of the University of Hawai’i at Mānoa, as set forth in the Student Conduct Code.

Title IX

UHM is committed to a free and safe environment from all forms of sex discrimination and gender-based violence. Campus resources, including confidential ones like CTAHR, are available for support and assistance.
